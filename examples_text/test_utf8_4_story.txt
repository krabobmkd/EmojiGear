🌆 Chapter 4: The City

The streets were alive:
  🚗 🚕 🚙 🚌 🚎 🏍 🚲 🛴 🛵
Cars honked 📯, cyclists rang bells 🔔, pigeons 🐦 cooed.
A street musician 🎸 played a tune – coins 🪙 flew into the case.
The smell of 🌮 tacos drifted from a food cart nearby.

💼 Chapter 5: At the Office

The team was already there:
  👩‍💻 Maria – debugging some gnarly 🐛 bug
  👨‍🎨 Tom – designing new 🎨 icons
  👩‍🔬 Sarah – analysing 📊 data
  🧑‍🚀 Dev – dreaming of 🚀 space (as usual)

"Good morning! ☕?" said Alex, holding up the coffee pot ☕.
Everyone cheered: 🎉🎉🎉

🎯 Chapter 6: The Meeting

The presentation started at 10:00 🕙:
  Slide 1: 📈 Sales up 42% – 🥳
  Slide 2: 📉 Costs down 18% – 😎
  Slide 3: 🔮 Forecast: ☀ sunny
  Slide 4: 💡 New ideas – 🤔💭
  Slide 5: ❓ Questions?

Everyone was impressed 🤩. The boss gave a 👍 👍 👍.
Alex felt proud 💪 and relieved 😅.

🌇 Chapter 7: Evening

After work 🏢, Alex passed the park 🌳🌳🌳.
Children 👧👦 played, dogs 🐕 ran, couples 👫 strolled.
The sky turned 🟠🔴🟣 as the sun set 🌅→🌃.

At home 🏠:
  🛁 Long bath
  🍜 Hot noodle soup
  📺 One episode of a favourite show 🎬
  📖 A few pages of a good book
  😴 Zzzz…
